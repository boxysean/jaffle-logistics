THE STORM WEEKWEEK 3 · 13-19 JAN 2025 · PARIS

What happened in Paris, what it cost, and what changed before the next winter
DETAILS
WHAT HAPPENED

A winter storm hit Paris 14-16 January; a truck jackknifed on an icy on-ramp (driver OK).

WHAT IT COST

€879.25 in service credits refunded to customers.

SLA

Paris ran January at 56.60% on time, against a 97% target.

LEARNINGS
  • Added warnings of upcoming storms.
  • Added an agent to detect an increase in tickets, raise an internal service incident, and begin proactive communications with impacted clients.
