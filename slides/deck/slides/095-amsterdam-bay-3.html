Not weather, not the fleet: handling damage at one dock bay
THE ISSUE
DAMAGE INCIDENTS · HUB-AMS
  • Not the fleet, drivers or freight: vehicles and drivers within ordinary variation, an ordinary maintenance record, the hub's own freight mix.
  • A place: bay 3 had no damage in 2023-24; its staging dwell rose from ~46 to 70 min.
  • Leading explanation: a dock-staging process fault, at moderate confidence.
THE IMPACT
  • The second contributing factor in Jaffle Werke's 2025 on-time decline, beside the Paris storm (CRM review, 20 Oct).

  • Bay 3 under audit, peak volume kept off it (#dispatch, 24 Nov).
  • Not measured: damage cost in euros.
  • Damage rose at four of five hubs in 2025: bay 3 is not the whole network's rise.
