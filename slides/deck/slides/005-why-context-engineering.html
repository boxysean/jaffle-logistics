WHY CONTEXT ENGINEERING

The case for modelling the business once, before anything reads it
THE CASE
  • AI rolled out to every employee in a year, and the context those tools read never rolled in
  • Nobody reviews an agent's every step
  • Ten wires, one layer: each of those tools wired itself straight to raw
PAID FOR ONCE
Unengineered context gets paid for three times.
Engineered context is paid for once.
  1. Paid once to wire the raw data again for each tool;
  2. paid again to keep that wiring correct as the sources move;
  3. and paid a third time by whoever acts on an answer that was wrong.
