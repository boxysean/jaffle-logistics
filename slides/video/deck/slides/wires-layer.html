WITH ONE CONTEXT LAYER
The same apps and the same five AI apps as the slide before, now organized, with one layer between them. The column ends in an ellipsis: it is every source the business has, not just these. The sources are wired in once: structured data from the CRM, the ERP and the spreadsheets, and unstructured data from the docs, contracts, email, chat, tickets and call recordings. The semantic layer owns the structured data: 10 governed metrics, so on_time_rate means one thing everywhere. The context layer owns the unstructured data: chunked, embedded and cited back to its source. Every AI app reads the layer, not the source. You pay once to wire it, and when a source moves you fix it in one place, not in every app. Nobody reviews an agent's every step, so the meaning has to be right before the agent reads it.
