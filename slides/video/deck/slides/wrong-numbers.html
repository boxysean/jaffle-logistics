This is the next slide, as an AI with raw table access and no semantic layer would draw it. Let it sit for a beat: it looks like a real board slide. Revenue over a hundred million, up 8%, on time above the contract every year. Then click three times. One: revenue. The AI joined the revenue ledger to shipments on client and month, so each monthly revenue row repeats once per shipment, about 35 times the truth. The real FY2025 figure is EUR 2,879,732. Two: on time, the dangerous one. It counted every shipment that was not failed as on time, so delayed and returned shipments pass, and 98.95% beats the 97% contract. The truth is 93.28%, a miss. Three: credits, the same bad join, 37 times the real EUR 52,955. Every number here is a real query against the raw source files. The first gets a laugh; the second gets a board to relax when it should be worried. The semantic layer defines revenue and on_time_rate once, so no tool gets to guess. Next slide: the real numbers.
