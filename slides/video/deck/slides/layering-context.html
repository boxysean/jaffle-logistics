On 16 January 2025 the safety team filed IR-9001 for incident INC-2025-00417: during the 14 to 16 January storm a semi jackknifed on an icy on-ramp leaving the Paris hub, no injuries. It arrives as free text, so parsing here means shaping, not OCR: staging restores its line breaks and int_incident_reports_documents joins it to its shipment, which gives it an account, Jaffle Werke. split_incident_reports cuts it into 13 sentences and chunk_incident_reports packs them into 3 token-bounded passages that never span two reports. incident_reports_embed turns each passage into a vector with embed(), and checks each passage's content hash so one that has not changed is not embedded and paid for again. knowledge_base lands them as 3 rows with source_type Incident Report, the account, the text, the embedding and a citation back to IR-9001. None of the functions are the hard part: Snowflake, Databricks and BigQuery each ship parse, chunk, embed and search as SQL functions. The per-warehouse table is in the appendix. The engineering is the pipeline between the calls.
