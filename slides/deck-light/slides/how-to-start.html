How to start
1
PICK THE QUESTION
The one your team asks again and again
Answer it in the repo: definition, grain, an owner.
2
WRITE THE CAVEAT
Next to the number

3
EXPOSE IT OVER MCP
Then read what comes back

Make this concrete and small. One: the question that keeps being re-asked in your team's Slack. The one your team asks again and again with a definition, a grain and an owner. Two: write the caveat next to the number, as a description or a test, so whoever reads it, person or agent, inherits the doubt. Three: expose the modelled, documented layer over MCP and audit what comes back: read the answers, trace them to lineage, fix the context wherever it was wrong. It is the same loop as a bad dashboard, with a faster consumer.
