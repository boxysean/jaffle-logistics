Walk left to right, one stage per click. Twelve operational systems, seeded as 26 source files in this demo (not connected). Fivetran lands them raw; dbt models 24 staging, 3 intermediate and 8 mart models; on top sit 25 context models (23 of them AI) and 10 governed metrics, exposed over MCP. One nightly job rebuilds everything from source, and a failing test stops the release. The detailed version of this slide is in the appendix.
