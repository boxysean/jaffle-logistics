EVERY TOOL DECIDES WHAT IT MEANS
This is the size of the problem, before the fix. On the left, where a company actually keeps what it knows: the CRM, the ERP, wiki pages, docs, spreadsheets, presentations, legal contracts, email, chat, support tickets, the HR system, call recordings. On the right, the AI apps and workflows everyone is building: a chat assistant, a BI copilot, a support agent, a sales agent, a forecast workflow. Each one wires itself straight to the sources it needs and decides for itself what a field means. Some of the wires are already broken: a source moved and nobody told the app. And some meaning is in no system at all. The definition of on time sits in a contract's credit terms; which of a client's contracts is the standing one lives in someone's head. That is how you get the 98.95% from earlier.
