Leading explanation: a dock-staging process fault (moderate confidence)
This is the second contributing factor in Jaffle Werke's 2025 on-time decline, beside the Paris storm (CRM review, 20 Oct). Vehicles and drivers are within ordinary variation, the maintenance record is ordinary, and it is the hub's own freight mix: the fleet is not the cause. It is a place. Bay 3 had no damage in 2023-24 and its staging dwell rose from about 46 to 70 minutes. Confidence is moderate, and the thing that would settle it is bay 3's equipment-inspection results. From Q4 on-time is tracked by cause and the hub runs an SOP audit; peak volume is kept off bay 3. Not measured: damage cost in euros. Damage rose at four of five hubs in 2025, so bay 3 is not the whole network's rise. The sources are messy: 14 of 15 incident reports name dock staging, and 5 reports cite IR-9003, 4 of them filed months before it existed, so free text needs provenance, not just retrieval.
